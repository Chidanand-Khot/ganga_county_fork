Understanding Section 194-IA for Plot Investments

When investing in high-value real estate such as larger residential villa plots or combined units at Ganga County where the total transaction consideration equals or exceeds Rs. 50 Lakh, statutory tax regulations mandate the deduction of Tax Deducted at Source (TDS). Developed by Atomoney in Garhmukteshwar, Ganga County ensures complete legal transparency, making it vital for buyers to understand their tax obligations under Section 194-IA of the Indian Income Tax Act.

Unlike regular salary TDS handled by employers, property TDS is the direct statutory responsibility of the buyer. Whether you are purchasing your plot near the NH-9 and Ganga Expressway corridor or reviewing your customized price quotation, mastering the 1% TDS rule ensures a penalty-free, smooth registration process.

Key TDS Compliance Highlights

  • Applicable Threshold: Mandatory for any immovable property transaction valued at Rs. 50 Lakh or higher.
  • Deduction Rate: Exactly 1% of the total sale consideration (or stamp duty value, whichever is higher).
  • Who Pays: The buyer must deduct 1% before releasing the final payment to the seller and deposit it via Form 26QB.
  • Time Limit: Must be deposited within 30 days from the end of the month in which the deduction was made.

Step-by-Step Procedure to Pay 1% TDS via Form 26QB

Complying with Indian tax laws for your property purchase at Ganga County involves four simple steps:

Step 1: Gather Details

Collect PAN card numbers, permanent addresses, and contact details of both the buyer(s) and seller(s), along with property details.

Step 2: File Form 26QB

Log into the Income Tax e-filing portal, navigate to e-File > e-Pay Tax, and fill out Form 26QB with accurate transaction amounts.

Step 3: Make Net Banking Payment

Pay the 1% TDS amount securely via net banking, debit card, or authorized bank challan. Save the generated acknowledgment receipt.

Step 4: Download Form 16B

Within 7 to 10 days of payment, download Form 16B from the TRACES portal and hand it over to the seller as proof of TDS deposit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding TDS deductions for property purchases at Ganga County:

Is TDS applicable when purchasing a residential plot above 50 Lakh at Ganga County?

Yes. Under Section 194-IA of the Income Tax Act, if the total consideration for a property purchase is Rs. 50 Lakh or more, the buyer is required to deduct 1% TDS before making the payment to the seller.

Who is responsible for deducting and depositing TDS on property purchases?

The buyer of the property is solely responsible for deducting 1% TDS and depositing it with the Income Tax Department via Form 26QB within 30 days from the end of the month in which the deduction was made.

What happens if there are multiple buyers or sellers for a plot at Ganga County?

If there are multiple buyers or sellers, separate Form 26QB challans must be filed for each buyer-seller combination reflecting their respective share of consideration.
