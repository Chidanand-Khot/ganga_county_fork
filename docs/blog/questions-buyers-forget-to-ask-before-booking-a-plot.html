The 6 Critical Questions Every Buyer Frequently Forgets to Ask

Make sure you have clear, written answers to these vital questions before making your booking payment:

1. What is the Exact Land Title & Conversion Status?

Always confirm whether the land is freehold, agricultural-to-residential converted, and free from any legal litigation or ancestral mortgage claims.

2. What are the Internal Road Widths & Access Specs?

Verify that internal roads are wide enough (e.g., 30 to 40 feet) to accommodate two-way traffic, fire tenders, and heavy construction vehicles easily.

3. Are Utilities Underground or Overhead?

Modern townships feature underground electrical cabling, storm drains, and sewage lines to prevent clutter and ensure uninterrupted utility supply.

4. What Additional Costs Apply at Registry?

Beyond the basic sale price, inquire about stamp duty, registration fees, maintenance deposits, and PLC charges as outlined in our GST and hidden charges guide.

5. What are the Boundary Wall & Security Provisions?

Check if the township is fully gated with perimeter boundary walls, 24/7 security guards, and CCTV surveillance to safeguard your investment.

6. When is the Expected Possession & Construction Timeline?

Understand the exact phase delivery schedule, allowing you to plan your dream villa construction timeline without unexpected delays.
